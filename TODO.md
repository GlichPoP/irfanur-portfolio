# Portfolio Decisions and Status

This document records the architectural and design decisions finalized for Irfanur Rahman's portfolio website ([https://irfanurrahman.vercel.app](https://irfanurrahman.vercel.app)).

---

## 1. Resume and CV Access (Resolved)
- **Decision:** Keep the full CV off the public web.
- **Rationale:** Protects personal sensitive data (phone number, home address) and encourages interested recruiters and hiring managers to make direct contact via the interactive email copy button or LinkedIn.

---

## 2. PROBAHO CRM Showcase (Resolved)
- **Decision:** Minimalist presentation using the high-resolution desktop command-center preview frame.
- **Rationale:** Keeps page load times instantaneous, eliminates unnecessary galleries, and highlights the core technical capabilities (offline first, SQLite WebAssembly, courier routing) with zero maintenance.

---

## 3. Scope of Work and Research (Resolved)
- **Decision:** Fixed, permanent core showcase consisting of:
  1. **PROBAHO CRM Solutions** (Flagship desktop software tool)
  2. **FIN423:** Portfolio Optimization and Efficient Frontier Simulation (38 pages)
  3. **FIN433:** Equity Research and Valuation Modeling of Square Pharmaceuticals Ltd (48 pages)
  4. **FIN422:** Valuation Analysis and Industry Research of Bangladesh Pharmaceuticals (22 pages)
- **Rationale:** Delivers the highest possible signal-to-noise ratio for corporate finance, equity valuation, and supply chain logistics roles without cluttering the page.

---

## 4. Permanent Domain Strategy (Resolved)
- **Canonical Production URL:** `https://irfanurrahman.vercel.app`
- **Application Alignment:** Standardised for direct inclusion on CVs, cover letters, and multinational management trainee application portals.
