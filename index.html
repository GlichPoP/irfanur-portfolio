Portfolio Optimization and Efficient Frontier Simulation

My role: Responsible for Grameenphone valuation, covariance matrix formulation, and 34 portfolio efficient frontier simulation.

The group evaluated risk, return and covariance relationships across four Dhaka Stock Exchange equities over 250 trading days.

The group simulated 34 portfolio weight combinations to construct the Markowitz efficient frontier and identify the optimal risk return allocation.

14.25% Optimum portfolio risk (Group result)
