About Me

Unifying corporate finance with software technology to build high-performance business tools.

I graduated with a BBA in Finance and Minor in Supply Chain Management from BRAC University, complemented by hands-on corporate accounting experience managing Oracle ERP general ledger entries at Square Toiletries Ltd.

My focus is bridging finance and supply chain operations to eliminate corporate bottlenecks. Rather than viewing financial reconciliation and warehouse logistics as separate silos, I understand how inventory discrepancies, courier Cash-on-Delivery (COD) cycles, and procurement lead times directly constrain working capital.

By directing modern AI systems and software technology with domain insight, I architect and build practical tools—such as PROBAHO CRM Solutions—that ensure showroom and enterprise checkouts execute without friction.
