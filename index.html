PROBAHO CRM Solutions

Offline-First Enterprise Business Management & Retail Showroom Operations Platform

The Problem

Retail showrooms and distributors frequently lose sales and disrupt order fulfillment during internet outages. Generic cloud CRMs suffer high latency, unpredictable downtime, and leak sensitive customer margins to third-party cloud providers.

The Architecture

Architected an offline-first system using SQLite via WebAssembly (sql.js) wrapped in Electron and React 19. Data writes execute with sub-millisecond local speed, guaranteeing 100% operational uptime without an active internet connection.

Key Engineering Achievements:

  • In-App Background Auto-Updater: Engineered a zero-cost distribution pipeline that downloads binary updates in the background with live percentage streaming and automated 1-click relaunch.
  • Adaptive Multi-Region Logistics: Built pre-configured delivery engines covering all 64 districts in Bangladesh with courier dispatch profiles (Pathao, Steadfast, RedX) and COD payment reconciliations.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Separated administrative owners from operational staff with signed audit trails (created_by, last_modified_by).
  • Thermal Challan & Invoicing: Automated printable invoice generator with barcodes, branded watermarks, and automatic RTO return-risk customer protection.
