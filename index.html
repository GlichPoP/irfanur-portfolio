PROBAHO CRM Solutions

Offline-First Enterprise Business Management & Retail Showroom Operations Platform

The Operational Challenge

Retail showrooms and multi-branch distributors frequently lose sales and disrupt order fulfillment during network outages. Cloud-only CRMs suffer high latency, risk data leakage, and lack automated reconciliation with local courier workflows.

The Engineering Architecture

Designed and built an offline-first system using SQLite via WebAssembly (sql.js) embedded in Electron and React 19. All data transactions write to local disk in sub-milliseconds, guaranteeing uninterrupted operations regardless of internet connectivity.

Key Architectural & Business Features:

  • In-App Background Auto-Updater: Zero-cost delivery pipeline with silent background downloads, percentage streaming, and 1-click relaunch via GitHub CDN.
  • 64-District Logistics Engine: Native courier dispatch profiles (Pathao, Steadfast, RedX) with real-time COD reconciliation and return-risk shields.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Granular separation of administrative owners and POS staff with cryptographic audit trails.
  • Thermal Invoicing & Barcodes: Automated printable invoice generator with branded watermarks, QR codes, and sales ledger accounting.
