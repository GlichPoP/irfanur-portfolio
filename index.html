The Operational Friction

Retail showrooms and regional distributors throughout Bangladesh face frequent broadband outages. Standard cloud-only CRMs stall cash drawers, freeze customer billing at the counter, and lack localized courier Cash-on-Delivery (COD) reconciliation engines.

⚙️

The Architectural Blueprint

Architected an offline-first execution environment leveraging SQLite WebAssembly (sql.js) embedded in Electron 43 and React 19. All sales, inventory audits, and invoice generations happen on-device in sub-milliseconds, guaranteeing 100% operational uptime without an internet connection.

📈

Zero checkout downtime, instant barcode scanner recognition, fully automated COD financial reconciliation against Bangladesh courier logs (Pathao, Steadfast, RedX), and automated background app updates delivered silently via GitHub CDN.
