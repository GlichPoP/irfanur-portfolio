PROBAHO CRM Solutions

Offline-First Enterprise Business Management & Retail Showroom Operations Platform

The Problem I Wanted to Solve

Retail showrooms and distributors frequently struggle when internet connections drop. Cloud-only CRMs halt sales operations and lack localized courier workflow integrations for courier companies in Bangladesh.

How I Built the Solution

As a tech enthusiast with a passion for software craftsmanship, I taught myself and implemented an offline-first architecture using SQLite via WebAssembly (sql.js) embedded inside Electron and React 19, giving users sub-millisecond local speed without needing internet.

Key Capabilities Built into the App:

  • In-App Background Auto-Updater: Engineered a zero-cost update pipeline using GitHub Releases with real-time download percentage streaming and 1-click relaunch.
  • 64-District Logistics Engine: Configured dispatch templates for Pathao, Steadfast, and RedX with cash-on-delivery (COD) reconciliation.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Separated administrative owner accounts from sales staff with audit activity logging.
  • Thermal Invoicing & Barcodes: Automated printable invoice generator with branded watermarks and product barcodes.
