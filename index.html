PROBAHO CRM Solutions is a purpose-built desktop application designed for Bangladesh's fast-moving retail showrooms, apparel chains, and wholesale distributors. In emerging markets where broadband interruptions routinely crash cloud-only POS systems, PROBAHO delivers 100% operational uptime with zero dependence on constant cloud connections.

Engineered with Electron 43 and React 19, PROBAHO embeds an in-memory and local-file SQLite 3 WebAssembly engine via sql.js that executes inventory deductions, billing lookups, and customer accounts with sub-millisecond (< 1ms) query latency.
